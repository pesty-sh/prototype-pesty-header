Mouse, motion, and the wordmark.

The letters are a bitmap in a Rust field. Move and the nearest cells take the light. Hold, and an icon charges. Release, and it appears, slides, or expands. Click the word and the next appear plays.

  • Move wakes the nearest cells
  • Hold charges an icon
  • Release lets that icon run
  • The word plays the next appear

Pointer, motion, and the logo share a single RGBA buffer. This header is that buffer.

Pointer

Where the cursor sits, how long the press has been held, and whether it landed on the letters. The bar and the sentence are quiet rectangles, so the type stays put.

02

Motion

The mark prints from the left on the first load. Each click runs the next appear. A release drops one icon. It may appear in place, slide away, or expand until it fades. Reduced motion holds the mark finished and the field still.

03

Logo

The word is a bitmap, crest on the ascenders and dim on the tails. The demo redraws that bitmap from a title, a face, and two hex colours.
